How do I tickle Mallow?

Rub a finger gently back and forth across the mint tummy. Mallow needs movement to giggle; holding a finger still is not tickling. On a laptop, hold the left mouse button and rub.

How do I tap a foot, cheek, or the head?

Use a short tap with very little movement. A long press or a drag is treated differently and may not produce the tap reaction.

Why does a toy button sometimes not react?

A character-interaction finger and a UI finger are separate. If a finger is already rubbing Mallow, a second finger can use the toy buttons. Starting a finger on the UI never falls through to the character.

How do I change sound, music, haptics, or motion?

Open the gear button → Settings. Sound and Music cycle through 0–100%; Haptics, Reduced motion, and tutorial replay are there too.

Does the game need internet or an account?

No. It is fully offline, has no accounts, ads, analytics, or purchases, and stores preferences only on the device.

Is iOS haptics supported?

Not yet. Android supports optional gentle haptics. iOS currently runs silently for haptics; the setting is harmless.

How do I reset the tutorial or local data?

Settings → Replay tutorial. To reset all local preferences, uninstall/reinstall or clear the app’s data from the platform settings.

Is Mallow a real child or an existing toy?

No. Mallow is an original fictional creature. The character artwork is original. Child laughter uses a CC0 recording by medyk3D (Freesound sound 616088); music and other short reactions are synthesized.
