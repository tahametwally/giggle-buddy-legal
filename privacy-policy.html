Giggle Buddy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · App: Giggle Buddy

Summary

Giggle Buddy is an offline virtual-plush toy. It does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information the app stores

The app stores a small set of preferences locally on your device: sound-effects volume, music volume, whether haptics are enabled, whether reduced motion is enabled, and whether the tutorial has been completed. These values use the platform’s local preference storage and are not sent to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or accounts
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data
  • Purchase history or payment information
  • Analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics
  • User-generated content, chat, or messages

Network and permissions

The release build is designed to work fully offline. The Android build requests only the normal VIBRATE permission for optional haptics; it does not request the INTERNET permission. The iOS build does not request camera, microphone, photo, location, contacts, or tracking permissions. If a future version adds network, analytics, ads, accounts, or other processing, this policy and the store privacy declarations will be updated before release.

Children’s privacy

Giggle Buddy is a fictional character and does not depict a real child. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game is intended for children ages 3–8. If you believe the app has unexpectedly collected personal information, contact taha.metwally94@gmail.com.

Third-party components

The app includes the Unity runtime and open-source/font components described in the project’s THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES file. It does not include advertising, analytics, or social SDKs.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if data practices change; the effective date will be revised. Contact Taha Metwally, taha.metwally94@gmail.com.
